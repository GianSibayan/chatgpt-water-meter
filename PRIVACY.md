# Privacy

ChatGPT Water Meter runs entirely in your browser.

- It reads the text of your messages and ChatGPT's replies on chatgpt.com only to estimate token counts. That text is never stored or sent anywhere.
- It saves running totals (water, energy and CO2 estimates per day, and per chat using the chat ID from the page address) in your browser's local extension storage.
- It makes no network requests, has no analytics, and shares nothing with anyone.
- Removing the extension deletes everything it saved.

Not affiliated with OpenAI.
